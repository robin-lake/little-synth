Complete Pinout (PCM5122 → Teensy 4.1)

  Power:
  - VIN → Teensy 3.3V
  - GND → Teensy GND

  I2C Control:
  - SDA → Pin 18 (SDA)
  - SCL → Pin 19 (SCL)

  I2S Audio:
  - BCK → Pin 21 (BCLK)
  - DIN → Pin 7 (OUT1A)
  - MCK → Pin 23 (MCLK)

  Digital Control Pins:
  - MUTE → Pin 2
  - DEEM → Pin 3
  - FILT → Pin 4
  - ATT0 → Pin 5
  - MOD1 → Pin 6
  - MOD2 → Pin 8
  - AGN → Pin 9
